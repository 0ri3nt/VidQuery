## Blockers and risks

- **Non-deterministic indexing — fixed 26 Oct (new indexes only)**: Whisper now decodes greedily at temperature 0 by default (`WHISPER_TEMPERATURE_FALLBACK=false`; `true` restores the old sampling fallback). Segment metadata records `whisper_decoding`. **[measured]** 40 Charades clips × 3 repeats: 40/40 identical (also across processes), against 17/40 with the fallback. Two full pipeline rebuilds of 20 test videos: 131/131 segments identical in every field, against 66/131 transcript, 45 action and 54 relation differences between the earlier fallback rebuilds (`evaluation/results/whisper-determinism-2026-10-26/`). Existing indexes (all Charades and AVA benchmark indexes) were built with the fallback and were not rebuilt, so their numbers still carry the ~±1.5 P@1 rebuild noise.

- **In-domain non-answerable queries**: no threshold separates them; needs a verifier that checks the specific action–object combination (e.g. a clip-level video-language model), not calibration.
- **Ranking ceiling**: single-frame OpenCLIP cannot see verbs or order; next step is a clip-level encoder or a moment-retrieval head, which means new dependencies (approval needed).
- **Lecture benchmark**: still needs recordings and human labels; speech/OCR/phonetic claims stay unmeasured.
- **Relation model in existing indexes**: AVA and Charades indexes carry v2 relation evidence; reprocessing is optional and not started.
